Do I need prior AI experience?

No. You need Python basics. A 4-hour Python primer is sent on enrolment, before Sunday 1.

What laptop do I need?

16 GB RAM is recommended. GPU work runs on Google Colab, Kaggle Notebooks or cloud credits, so a gaming GPU is not needed.

What if I miss a Sunday?

You still submit that Sunday's project on GitHub. Certification needs 5 of 6 Sundays attended.

Why submit on GitHub every week?

Your tagged GitHub repository becomes the portfolio that recruiters and founders look at. The certificate prints its link.

Will this get me a job at a top AI company?

It gives you a strong, portfolio-backed start and the skills top teams test. Offers also depend on practice and work you keep building after the course. A 90-day post-course plan is included.

An honest note on outcomes. AlgoProfessor gives mentoring and referrals where it can, but does not guarantee placement.
